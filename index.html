NZ POWER MARKET EXPERTISE

Your electricity retailer matters.

The electricity retailer you choose has a major impact on the total financial return of your solar investment. In New Zealand, retailers vary wildly in their solar buy-back rates, daily fixed charges, and off-peak tariffs.

1
Surplus Solar Buy-Back (Export Tariff): Rates range from as low as 8c/kWh up to 17c/kWh across NZ power companies. A higher buy-back rate dramatically accelerates your system payback.
2
Off-Peak & Night Rates: If you have battery storage or an EV, selecting a retailer with low overnight tariffs allows you to charge cheaply during winter months when sun is limited.
3
Daily Fixed Connection Charges: Low daily charge plans protect homeowners who generate almost all their own power from being penalized by high fixed meter fees.
💡
The Solar Shop Difference: We don't just bolt panels to your roof and walk away. As part of our assessment, we evaluate your bill and recommend the exact electricity retailer plan in your region that maximizes your return.

Choosing the right electricity retailer

Profile A

High Daytime Self-Consumption

If your household or farm uses most power during the day, choose retailers offering the lowest daytime kWh consumption rate and lowest daily fixed charge (e.g. Contact, Genesis, Mercury).

Profile B

High Grid Export & Battery Storing

If you are away during the day and export surplus energy, prioritize retailers offering premium buy-back rates of 13c to 17c per kWh (e.g. Octopus Energy, Ecotricity).
